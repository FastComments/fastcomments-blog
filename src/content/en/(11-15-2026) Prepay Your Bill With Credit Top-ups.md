# [postlink]Prepay Your Bill With Credit Top-ups[/postlink]

{{#unless isPost}}
You can now add credit to your FastComments account from the billing page. Credit is applied to your future invoices, including renewals and Flex usage, before your card is charged.
{{/unless}}

{{#isPost}}

### What's New

Until now there was one way to pay for FastComments: we charged your card when an invoice came due. That's fine for
most accounts, but not all of them. Some finance departments would rather make one payment and be done for a while.
Some Flex customers would rather pay for a busy month ahead of time than be surprised by it.

Now you can put money on the account. The billing page has a new **Manage Credits** button, next to **View Invoices** and **Invoice
Settings**. It opens a **Credits** page where you can see your balance and add to it.

Credit is money held on your FastComments account, in your billing currency. When an invoice is created, your credit
is applied first, and only the remainder, if any, is charged to your card. That covers subscription renewals, Flex
usage charges, and anything else we invoice you for. There's nothing to configure and nothing to remember; once the
balance is there, it's used.

### Adding Credit

1. Open <a href="https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-info" target="_blank">My Account -> Billing Info</a> and click **Manage Credits**.
2. Under **Add credit**, pick one of the preset amounts (10, 25, 50, or 100 in your currency) or choose **Other amount** and type your own, anywhere from 5 to 10,000 per top-up.
3. Click **Add**. Your saved card is charged, and if your bank wants you to confirm the payment, that happens right there.
4. The page shows **Credit added** with your new balance.

Every top-up is listed in the **Past top-ups** table on the same page, with its date, amount, and whether it was
**Credited**, **Pending**, or **Failed**. If a card is declined, nothing is charged and the balance doesn't change.

Only the account owner or a user with the **Billing Admin** permission can add credit, the same people who can change
the plan or the payment method today.

### The Details

Credit isn't a discount, a points system, or a separate currency. It's your money, in your currency, sitting on your
account until an invoice uses it. The balance shows on the billing page whenever it's above zero, and each invoice
shows the credit that was applied to it.

Top-ups use the payment method already saved on your account, so there's no second checkout to set up. If you haven't
saved a card yet, the Credits page points you to the billing page to add one first.

Accounts billed through Shopify or BigCommerce, and accounts that are invoiced outside the dashboard, are paid on
those platforms instead, so they won't see the **Manage Credits** button.

### Documentation

<a href="https://docs.fastcomments.com/guide-billing.html#credits" target="_blank">The new Billing guide</a> covers

If you've wanted to prepay, keep a buffer against usage charges, or make your accounting department's month easier,
